Original

Вибери свет

„Разве жизнь прекрасна ?” –Скажет человек.И вопреки себе, напрасно,Ответит – «Нет».

Безусловно, жить во тьмеНе есть краса,Ведь жизнь без Бога –Сплошная тьма.

Бог есть слово,Слово – есть свет,Кто понимает слово,Не ответит – «Нет».

1Oleg Schtereb

Translation

Choose the Light

"Is life really beautiful?" —a man will say.And against himself, in vain,he'll answer — "No."

Without a doubt, to live in darknessis no beauty,for life without Godis unbroken darkness.

God is the word,the word is light;whoever understands the wordwill not answer — "No."

About the poem

The title is a Ukrainian verb with a Russian noun — the author’s two languages in three words. “Is life beautiful?” a person asks, and “against himself, in vain,” answers no. Life without God is “total darkness”; “God is the word, the word is light,” and whoever understands the word will not say no. Don’t hide, don’t run; accept the word, “and do not perish.” Life is beautiful, “nothing is more beautiful” — and choosing death “puts out the bright light in vain.” A hymn in plain quatrains, and the collection’s clearest answer to its own darkest poems.
