Bright Light

To some I say good morning,My breath before the sun.The feeling, not so boring,Before the day’s begun.

To some I say goodnight,As light begins to fade.Let’s listen to the silenceWhere memory is made.

To some I say goodbye—No drama, no one traumatized.Just a vision in the silenceThat lingers in my heart.

1Oleg Shtereb

Bright Light

To some I am beginning.To some I am the end.A stillness shaped like knowing,Too close to comprehend.

To some I say good morning.To some I say goodnight.To some I say goodbye,And appear a bright light.

To some I say all three—Good morning, night, goodbye.The shadow of the memory,The light we can’t deny.
