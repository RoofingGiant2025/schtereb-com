Бред наркомана

Нельзя забыть тот теплый вечер,Когда большим было желанье встречи,Когда в глубокой тайне небесСверкали звезды от чудес.

Весь город обретал покой,Никто уже не сорился с женой.Догорали в окнах светлые огни,И куда-то подевались светлячки.

Ласково обняла наши телаСветом своим с неба луна.Тишина…Тишину сорвала муха,Та, что ужалила тебя в ухо.

1Oleg Schtereb

Translation

Junkie's Delirium

One can't forget that warm evening,when the wish to meet was great,when in the deep secret of the skiesthe stars sparkled with wonders.

The whole town was settling into calm;no one quarrelled with his wife any more.The bright lights in the windows burned down low,and the fireflies had gone off somewhere.

Tenderly the moon embraced our bodieswith its light down from the sky.Silence… The silence was torn by a fly —the one that stung you on the ear.

About the poem

A warm evening in a city at peace — “no one was quarrelling with his wife any more” — and two bodies under the moon, until a fly stings her ear. She goes wild “like a cat fallen from a high window,” claws out, and runs home; “little sister anguish” comes to sit with the speaker and shakes him awake; not liking to be alone, he runs after her. The title frames the anecdote as an intoxicated ramble; the poem itself is quick, comic and oddly tender.
