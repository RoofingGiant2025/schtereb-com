Don't Over…

translation · original: українська

Be a muse for me that sounds like music in gentle, tender, adjacent notes.

Be a flower for me that is an eternal spring; a heady, fragrant aroma — be a holiday for me.

Be a game for me that captivates, surge as an emotional stream, only don't be a shock.

Be a nanny for me, a toy and a plaything, read me the magazine "Maliatko," for I'm still a little child.

Be sweet for me, a sincere smile, but above all, with me, always be yourself.

"Maliatko" ("Little One") is a Ukrainian children's magazine.
