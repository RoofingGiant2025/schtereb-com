UNDERSTAND

translation · original: русский

Life is a game, but if it's a game where there's no shield and sword, then for me it isn't worth the candle!

The main thing in life isn't giving death to others and taking life away, but filling the emptiness of others' souls… with the perfect, and… healing…

But then what will help when the wish to live goes away, and it becomes impossible to forgive oneself.

Don't perish — pray! Seek and you shall find — paths that lead to where you'll understand that not everyone lies, and… you'll find the meaning of life.
