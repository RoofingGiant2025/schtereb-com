П’яна субота

Якось троє ми зібрались,На дворі темно вже було.На третій поверх ми добрались,І в пляшках витягли бухло .

Стаканчики приніс Андрій,Сказав Валері :”Йди помий.”Валера швидко їх помив,Й горілочку у них налив.

По першому ми враз бухнули,Й водичкою чим дужче запили.Час пройшов... Усе розлили,І сильно в нас мозги поплили.

1Oleg Schtereb

Translation

Drunken Saturday

One time the three of us got together;outside it was already night.Up to the third floor we climbedand pulled the bottles of booze out.

Andriy brought the little cups,told Valera: "Go wash them up."Valera washed them, quick as that,and poured the vodka into them.

The first one we knocked back at once,and chased it hard with water.Time passed… We'd poured the whole lot out,and our brains had drifted off.

About the poem

Three friends, a third-floor room, bottles from a bag. The comedy is in the detail: Andriy brings the cups and sends Valera to wash them; the first shot is chased with water; a cigarette is forbidden, then bought with another pour; “we very drunk junkies” tidily collect the butts and wash the cups before heading out. Beer by brand name, the disco, chewing gum against the vodka breath, a Sunday throat — and the assurance that next Saturday will bring “our own kind of work.” A chronicle in rhymed quatrains, sung rather than confessed.
