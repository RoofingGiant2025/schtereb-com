Я останусь один

Я останусь один,Я буду как ветер,Даже если придетсяСмертью ответить.

И ни что не имеет значения,Ни вчера, ни завтра.Ни общество, ни отречение,Когда умираешь внезапно.

Только одна душа,Что легко из телаВ пространство ушла,Оставив все то, что имела.

1Oleg Schtereb

Translation

I'll Stay Alone

I will stay alone,I will be like the wind,even if I haveto answer with death.

And nothing has any meaning,not yesterday, not tomorrow.Not society, not renunciation,when you die suddenly.

Only one soul,which lightly, from the body,has gone out into space,leaving all that it had.

About the poem

Short lines, a calm voice, and a decision: to stay alone and “be like the wind, even if I must answer with death.” Nothing matters — yesterday, tomorrow, society, renunciation — “when you die suddenly.” Only the soul, which leaves the body lightly into space; let it merge “as a drop and the ocean,” and no one will provoke deception any more. The image of the drop and the sea is old and mystical; the poem uses it not to mourn but to stop struggling.
