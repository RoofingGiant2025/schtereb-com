Entropy in a Poem

Every time I sit and write, it's entropy in the poem
Organized from scattered light
Every time I hear your voice, I sublimate the meaning of choice in life
Every line is a chaos dramatized, a negotiating gently conquer love and fate
From the cave, I breathe the world, I ride the wave
I build a temple of love, unfurled
Even disintegration calls, a whisper in the broken walls
Every fall a sacred sign, a perfect loss
By design, you speak
And I begin to bend, not to break but just transcend
There's structure in the soft collapse, truth revealed in thunderclaps
Entropy is not the end, I fear that
It's the shape of truth
When the veil is clear
For us to see it end
End
