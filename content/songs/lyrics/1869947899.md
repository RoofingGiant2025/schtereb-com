Walking by the ocean,
You decide to fly.
You are so free,
You are in your dreams.

Yes, you can fly
Like a butterfly
Flattered to flutter,
Always seem so shy.

Can you see the stars?
Yes, you surely can.
They are in your eyes,
Your eyes are full of them.

Look into the skies,
Into the bright realm,
See marvelous stars?
You are one of them.

Open your eyes wider,
I am going deeper
Into stunning art,
Deeper in your heart.

I can see the beauty —
It lives in your heart.
It’s a butterfly…
The butterfly is love.

Once wondering around,
Around the world,
Now dwells in your heart,
It’s your love.

Your love is true,
Your dreams are wild.
True love is you,
Never stopped by mind.

Fly, my butterfly...
